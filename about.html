About Muhammad Muaz

Head of Accounts · Finance Graduate · Accounting Educator

Hello, I'm Muaz

I'm Muhammad Muaz, Head of Accounts at Workman Services, an HVAC and MEP contracting company in Pakistan. I hold a B.Com (Hons) with a specialisation in Finance, and I teach practical accounting the way it is done in real Pakistani businesses.

In my role at Workman Services I work across the full accounting cycle of a project-based business: recording and reviewing transactions, managing the company's ERP and its chart of accounts, and preparing stock reports that I reconcile with the system every month.

I started Muaz Academy to share these practical skills. University teaches the theory; this site focuses on how accounting actually works inside a company – with the tools, documents and problems you will really meet at work.

What you'll find here

  • Courses on accounting fundamentals, inventory, ERP and chart of accounts, Pakistan tax basics and Excel.
  • Services for businesses that need help with bookkeeping, ERP setup, stock reporting or Excel tools.
  • Premium material such as templates and in-depth modules for learners who want to go further.
