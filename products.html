FAQ

Questions about Maju ERP

Is my company's data safe?

Your data is saved online in a secure database. Every company's data is kept separate, and the database itself stops anyone outside your company from seeing it. Only people you add with your company code can sign in, and you can switch their logins off at any time.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You'll see how to pay the monthly subscription. As soon as your payment is confirmed, your access continues with all your data exactly as you left it. Nothing is deleted.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Send the receipt on WhatsApp and your access is extended for the months you paid. You can see your payment history inside Maju ERP under Settings → Users & Subscription.

Can my whole team use it at the same time?

Yes. Each person has their own login, and if two people save at the same moment, Maju ERP combines both changes. The number of users depends on your plan.

Can you set it up for my business?

Yes. As a Chartered Accountant I can set up your chart of accounts, opening balances, items and tax settings, and train your staff. Ask for a quote.
