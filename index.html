About me

Common questions

How do your fees work?

Fees depend on the scope and volume of work. After a short discussion I send a fixed quote – a monthly fee for ongoing work or a one-time fee for returns and projects. No surprises.

Can you work with clients outside my city?

Yes. Most work is done online – documents are shared by WhatsApp or email, and we meet by call when needed.

What do I need for my income tax return?

Usually your CNIC, salary certificate or business records, bank statements, and withholding tax certificates. I send a simple checklist once we start.

Do you keep my information confidential?

Yes. Client information is kept strictly confidential and is used only for your work, in line with ICAP's code of ethics.

Which accounting software do you work with?

I work with ERP systems and common accounting software, as well as Excel-based books for smaller businesses.
