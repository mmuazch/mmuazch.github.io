Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muaz Academy ("we", "this website"), run by Muhammad Muaz, handles information when you visit https://mmuazch.github.io.

Information we collect

This website has no user accounts and does not ask for personal information to read lessons. When you use a contact or quote form, the form opens WhatsApp with your message; the information is only sent if you choose to send it in WhatsApp. We use it only to reply to you.

Course progress

Lesson progress is stored in your own browser (local storage) so the site can show which lessons you completed. It is not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Cookies and advertising

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at www.aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party services

The site uses Google Fonts and may embed YouTube videos (in privacy-enhanced mode). These services may receive technical data such as your IP address when content loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Payments

Premium access and contributions are paid by direct bank transfer. We never ask for card numbers or online banking passwords on this website.

Children

This website is intended for students and professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows the latest version. For questions, contact us.
